# Person 3 secondary Stage >=2 baseline contribution

## Methods

The secondary endpoint was observed creatinine-defined Stage 2 or 3 AKI within the same ascertainment window as the primary analysis. The existing aki_stage2_label and the full modelling population were retained: Stage 1 and operationally negative encounters were coded 0. This is not an analysis restricted to primary-positive encounters. The frozen patient-separated partitions contained 79,364 training encounters from 46,263 patients (1,589 positives) and 16,932 validation encounters from 9,913 patients (333 positives). No new split or data preprocessing was fitted for model inputs.

Four comparators were newly fitted to the secondary labels: training prevalence, unpenalised age/gender logistic regression (standardised age and male indicator), structured L2 logistic regression (27 transformed features), and structured-plus-text L2 logistic regression (27 structured features and 2,772 frozen chief-complaint TF-IDF terms). Person 2's existing lr_structured/lr_text preprocessing supplied training-median imputation, standardisation, eight missingness indicators and one-hot encoding. The text vocabulary, IDF and settings were unchanged and matched the shared tree_text representation. No XGBoost-specific sparse-zero correction is required for LR.

Each regularised model independently selected C from 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, and 100 by maximum secondary validation AP, with ties within 1e-12 favouring smaller C. Models used lbfgs, tol 1e-8 and iteration limits of 2,000 for structured and age/gender LR and 20,000 for text LR. All fits converged. Models were trained only on training data, without class weighting, resampling, train-plus-validation refitting or probability recalibration. No operating threshold was selected.

The selected C values were 100 for structured LR and 1 for structured-plus-text LR.

## Validation results (development estimates only)

| Model | AP | AUROC | Brier score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Training prevalence | 0.0197 | 0.5000 | 0.01928 |
| Age/gender LR | 0.0222 | 0.5486 | 0.01927 |
| Structured LR | 0.0430 | 0.6263 | 0.01914 |
| Structured + text LR | 0.0721 | 0.6659 | 0.01885 |

Validation prevalence was 1.9667%; the constant prediction was the training prevalence (2.0022%). Adding text within LR changed validation AP by +0.0291, AUROC by +0.0396, and Brier score by -0.00029.

The regularisation values were selected using this validation set. These are development estimates without patient-level confidence intervals, not independent test results. AP depends on outcome prevalence, so the secondary AP should not be directly compared with primary-outcome AP as evidence that one task is easier.

## Interpretation and evaluation status

This endpoint identifies observed disease in the selected outcome-assessable admitted population, including encounters already positive at the first sample. Operational negatives can include unobserved disease. Chief-complaint terms can reflect conditions already suspected at presentation; coefficients describe predictive associations and do not establish causality or anticipation before clinical suspicion.
